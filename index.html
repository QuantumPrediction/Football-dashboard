Method

From data to discipline

01

A committee, not a guru

Nine independent statistical models, retrained on two decades of match and market history, each estimate every fixture. No single opinion decides.

02

The market must agree

A signal is issued only when the committee consensus and the market price point the same way with conviction. ★ marks the highest-conviction tier.

03

A record that cannot be rewritten

Flat staking and an append-only ledger. Results use recorded recommendation prices; actual execution and publication timing require separate evidence.
